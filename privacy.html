Privacy Policy

What we access

Only the permission to upload videos to the connected TikTok account's drafts (scope video.upload). We do not read your profile, followers, messages or videos.

What we store

The access token TikTok returns is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never shared, sold or sent to anyone else.

Deleting

Revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings, Security, Apps and services. The stored token is then useless and deleted.
